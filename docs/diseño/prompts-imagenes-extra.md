# Prompts de imágenes — hojas extra

Complemento de [`prompts-imagenes-carta.md`](prompts-imagenes-carta.md). Mismas reglas:
foto realista **no muy grande y bien centrada, con margen de color alrededor**, sobre un
**fondo de color liso a sangre**, con reflejo, en rejilla, **sin texto**. Casillas
rectangulares (~3:2). Cada hoja trae su prompt y su lista de nombres para la herramienta
de recorte.

> **La única distinta es «Categorías»**: ahí NO es foto, es **icono plano tipo clip-art
> sobre fondo blanco** (para los botones de familia).

---

## 1 · Menús — rejilla 5×4 (20)

**Prompt:**

> High-end product catalog contact sheet, 16:9 canvas, **5 columns × 4 rows grid = 20 cells**. ## 5 · Sin gluten / veggie / vegano — rejilla 5×4 (20)

Las variantes se distinguen **por el color de fondo, siempre el mismo dentro de cada
dieta**: **azul apagado** = sin gluten (fila 1), **verde** = vegetariano (filas 2 y media
de la 3), **verde azulado (teal)** = vegano (resto). Así el camarero reconoce la dieta de
un vistazo, sin leer.

**Prompt:**

> High-end product catalog contact sheet, 16:9 canvas, **5 columns × 4 rows grid = 20 cells**. Every cell a **landscape rectangle wider than tall (~3:2), never square**, full-bleed; one realistic, appetizing, studio-lit dish photo per cell, **small and well-centered with a wide empty margin of solid color around it (fills about half the cell)**, with a soft reflection. **The background color encodes the diet and MUST stay identical within each diet**: gluten-free = the same muted blue, vegetarian = the same fresh green, vegan = the same teal. Adjacent cells touch, no gaps. **NO TEXT, letters, numbers, labels, logos or watermarks.**
> Cells (item — background): 1 gluten-free margherita pizza with basil — muted blue; 2 gluten-free bread rolls — muted blue; 3 gluten-free pasta with tomato — muted blue; 4 gluten-free chocolate brownie — muted blue; 5 gluten-free battered fish with fries — muted blue; 6 veggie burger with salad — green; 7 vegetable paella — green; 8 falafel with hummus — green; 9 grilled vegetable skewers — green; 10 caprese salad — green; 11 spinach & cheese quiche slice — green; 12 vegetable lasagna — green; 13 mushroom risotto — green; 14 vegan burger with lettuce — teal; 15 tofu & quinoa buddha bowl — teal; 16 vegan curry with rice — teal; 17 vegan poke bowl — teal; 18 plant-based nuggets with dip — teal; 19 vegan chocolate cake — teal; 20 dairy-free ice cream — teal.

## 6 · Categorías (fotos reales sobre blanco) — rejilla 6×5 (30)

Para los **botones de familia**: una **foto real** de un producto representativo de cada
categoría, **sobre fondo blanco** (limpio, a juego con las fotos de producto). El nombre lo
pone el TPV debajo.

**Prompt:**

> High-end product catalog contact sheet for a hospitality POS's category buttons, 16:9 canvas, **6 columns × 5 rows grid = 30 cells**. Every cell a **landscape rectangle wider than tall (~3:2), never square**, on a **plain pure-white background (studio white, seamless)**, with a soft natural shadow/reflection under the item. In each cell **ONE realistic, appetizing, studio-lit photo** of a representative food-or-drink item for that category, **small and well-centered with a wide white margin around it (the item fills only about half the cell)**. Same clean bright lighting and style across all 30 so they look like one family. Thin light-grey guideline between cells to cut. **NO TEXT, letters, numbers, labels, brand logos or watermarks — the item only.**
> Cells (category — item): 1 refrescos — a glass of cola with ice and a lemon slice; 2 cervezas — a draft beer with a foamy head; 3 cafés — a cup of café con leche with latte art; 4 vinos — a glass of red wine; 5 cócteles — a mojito with mint and lime; 6 licores — a glass of amber liqueur; 7 whiskys — a whisky tumbler with ice cubes; 8 chupitos — three colorful shot glasses in a row; 9 aguas — a bottle of mineral water and a glass; 10 zumos — a glass of fresh orange juice with orange halves; 11 tapas — a small plate of patatas bravas; 12 raciones — a plate of golden croquettes; 13 bocadillos — a filled baguette sandwich; 14 hamburguesas — a cheeseburger with fries; 15 pizzas — a whole margherita pizza with basil; 16 ensaladas — a fresh mixed salad in a bowl; 17 arroces — a small seafood paella pan; 18 pastas — a plate of spaghetti; 19 carnes — a grilled steak with grill marks; 20 pescados — a grilled fish fillet with lemon; 21 mariscos — a few grilled prawns; 22 desayunos — a breakfast set with coffee, toast and juice; 23 postres — a slice of cheesecake with berry jam; 24 helados — an ice-cream sundae; 25 menú — a small set-menu tray; 26 para llevar — a takeaway paper bag with food; 27 cafetería — a coffee cup next to a croissant; 28 combinados — a tall gin & tonic with ice and lime; 29 sin gluten — a couple of gluten-free bread rolls; 30 quesos — a wedge of cheese with grapes.
